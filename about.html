About snpick

Knowing the news isn't the same as understanding it.

snpick picks the world stories that matter, checks them against primary sources, and asks the question most coverage skips — what does this actually mean?

How we work

01

We pick

From hundreds of international stories a day, we choose the few that shape people's lives — not the ones that trend.

02

We check

Every claim is traced to a primary source. Every story ends with its sources listed — so you can check them too.

03

We take a stand

We don't pretend to be neutral. We write for human rights, fairness and accountability — and always label opinion as opinion.

Why this blog exists

International news usually arrives stripped of context. A war, an election or a trade deal gets a headline — but the thread connecting it to prices, jobs, migration and security at home gets lost.

snpick exists to rebuild that thread. It's a blog, not a newspaper — but it follows a newsroom's rules on sourcing and corrections.

Found an error? Tell us — every correction is published with a date.
